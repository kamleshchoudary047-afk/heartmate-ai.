Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Heartmate AI is an entertainment service featuring fictional AI companions. This policy explains what information the service processes and how it is used.

Information we process

  • Account information: Firebase/Google sign-in provides an account identifier and may provide your email address and display name.
  • Conversation and memory data: messages you send, AI replies, companion profile preferences, saved companion memories and relationship progress.
  • Feature data: voice preferences, notification preferences, daily-photo records and video-generation task identifiers.
  • Content you submit: prompts, text and audio submitted to use chat, transcription, image or video features.
  • Membership and payment records: selected VIP plan, membership status and expiry, Razorpay order ID, payment ID and payment amount. Card, UPI PIN and bank authentication details are processed by Razorpay and are not stored by Heartmate AI.
  • Technical data: information processed by the hosting and service providers to operate, secure and troubleshoot the application.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, provide AI conversations, restore chat history, personalize companion responses, maintain relationship progress, and provide requested voice, image and video features. We may also use technical logs to investigate errors and protect the service.

Service providers

Heartmate AI is hosted using Cloudflare Workers, Cloudflare D1 and Cloudflare AI. Google Firebase Authentication is used for sign-in. Razorpay processes VIP payments and checkout details. Video generation uses Kling when that feature is requested. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services and under their own terms and privacy policies.

AI-generated content

Companions are fictional AI characters, not real people. Messages and generated media are produced using automated systems. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers or other highly sensitive information to a companion.

Storage and retention

Conversation and feature records are stored in the application's database so features such as chat history and memory can work. They remain stored until deleted through the account-data deletion feature or otherwise removed by the service. Technical logs may have separate retention periods controlled by the hosting provider.

Your choices and deletion

You can stop using the service at any time. To delete your Heartmate AI application data, visit Delete account and follow the instructions. This application-data deletion does not currently delete the separate Google/Firebase sign-in identity; that identity must be removed through the relevant account provider.

Children

Heartmate AI is intended only for adults aged 18 or older. It is not designed for children.

Security

We use authentication and access controls intended to protect account data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your sign-in credentials private and report suspected account misuse to the service operator.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page. A dedicated privacy contact address has not yet been published; add a monitored support contact before opening the service to the public.
