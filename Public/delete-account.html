Delete your Heartmate AI data

This permanently deletes the Heartmate AI application data associated with the signed-in account, including conversations, saved memories, relationship progress, companion profiles, photo records, voice preferences, notification preferences, daily welcomes and video-task records.

Before you continue

  • This action cannot be undone.
  • Only the currently signed-in account's app data is deleted.
  • Your separate Google/Firebase sign-in identity is not deleted by this action.

Checking sign-in status…

After successful deletion, you will be signed out. To remove your Google account identity itself, use Google's account settings separately.
